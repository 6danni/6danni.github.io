About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Chicago, advised by Professor Raul Castro Fernandez. My research lies at the intersection of databases, human-computer interaction, data visualization, and AI-assisted data work. I am interested in how interactive data systems can automate workflows while supporting meaningful user involvement, careful evaluation, and informed decision-making.

Previously, I completed my undergraduate studies at Washington University in St. Louis, where I worked with Dr. Alvitta Ottley.
